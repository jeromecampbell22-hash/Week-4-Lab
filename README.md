Mediroza General Hospital Penetration Testing Project
Batch: B083
Week: 4
Client: Mediroza General Hospital
Target: https://medirozahospital.com
Engagement Type: Black-box penetration test
Duration: 5 days

Important Notice
This project is intended for a controlled educational environment only. Testing must be performed solely within the approved scope and under explicit written authorization from the system owner. Do not apply these techniques to any system without permission.

Confidentiality: Networkwalks confidential — authorised personnel only.

Scope and Rules
Test only the approved target domain.

Do not conduct social engineering.

Do not perform denial-of-service testing.

Do not test systems outside the agreed scope.

Work independently and protect all discovered information.

Do not share findings with other participants before the reveal session.

Project Objectives
Identify vulnerabilities in the target web infrastructure.

Demonstrate the real-world impact of authorized findings.

Retrieve the three designated confidential patient PDF lab reports.

Recover the contents of the encrypted files using authorized methods.

Identify the specified exposure involving employee salaries and shareholder details.

Produce a professional penetration-testing report.

Milestones
M1 — Initial Access
Conduct reconnaissance, identify exposed entry points, assess authentication and input handling, and obtain the three designated patient PDF lab reports.

Deliverable: Proof of authorized access and the three retrieved PDF files.

M2 — Data Extraction
Analyze the encryption used on each retrieved PDF and recover the contents through appropriate authorized methods.

Deliverable: Recovered contents of all three files with proof of successful access.

M3 — Critical Data Exposure
Analyze the retrieved material and file properties to identify the further server exposure specified in the exercise.

Tasks:

Find the salaries of all hospital employees.

Find the shareholder details of the hospital.

Deliverable: Documented evidence of the exposure and a readable summary of the confidential data uncovered.

M4 — Penetration Testing Report
Prepare a detailed professional report containing the following sections:

Executive Summary

Scope and Methodology

Findings and Proof of Exploitation

Risk Rating

Recommendations and Remediation

Deliverable: Complete report submitted to the instructor.

Evidence and Documentation
Maintain an organized evidence directory containing:

Reconnaissance notes.

Relevant screenshots.

Timestamps and test steps.

Retrieved files and hashes where appropriate.

Sanitized proof of findings.

Risk-rating rationale.

Remediation recommendations.

Avoid including unnecessary personal or medical information in shared documentation. Store sensitive evidence securely and restrict access to authorized personnel.

Suggested Report Contents
For every finding, record:

Finding title and unique identifier.

Affected asset or endpoint.

Description of the issue.

Conditions required for exploitation.

Reproduction steps, limited to authorized testing.

Evidence and business impact.

Severity rating: Critical, High, Medium, or Low.

Recommended remediation.

Retest requirements.

Completion Checklist
Written authorization and scope confirmed.

Target boundaries reviewed.

Reconnaissance documented.

Three designated PDF reports retrieved.

File encryption analyzed and contents recovered.

Critical data exposure documented.

Evidence securely stored and sanitized.

Findings assigned risk ratings.

Remediation recommendations prepared.

Final penetration-testing report completed.

All temporary access, test accounts, and artifacts handled according to the engagement rules.
